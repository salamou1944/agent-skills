## Time-critical sequence

1. **Account activation:** obtain real affiliate links. This is the only external dependency that cannot be truthfully automated from this repository.
2. **Tracking validation:** click-test the provider link and confirm attribution/dashboard visibility.
3. **First asset:** publish one high-intent problem/solution piece with clear affiliate disclosure.
4. **Measurement:** record clicks, registrations, paid conversions, and provider-confirmed commission.
5. **Decision gate:** after real signal, either scale the topic/market or kill it.
6. **Second offer:** activate Hostinger in parallel once the first measurement path is proven.

## Non-negotiable revenue rule

Credits, impressions, clicks, registrations, simulated events, or projected commissions are **not cash revenue**. Revenue is recorded only from a provider-confirmed commission event.

## Compliance gates

- No self-referrals.
- No fake accounts.
- No cookie stuffing.
- No spam.
- No misleading or guaranteed-income claims.
- Affiliate disclosure must be clear and close to the endorsement/link.
